=== Monthly bills

Enter what your provider billed for a month, and Kafka Cost Control shares it among topics and principals by their usage. Where no bill applies, the costs come from the pricing rules and are marked as estimated - so a running month shows estimates until its bill is in.

A bill has, in dollars:

* one amount per usage-billed line, named after the metric it is shared by: `confluent_kafka_server_request_bytes` (network write), `confluent_kafka_server_response_bytes` (network read), `confluent_kafka_server_retained_bytes` (storage) and `kafka_topic_partition_count` (partitions);
* optionally *other lines*: everything else on the bill (connectors, support, credits as a negative amount), each with a description and where it goes:
** *to a context*, e.g. `tenant=data-platform` or `application=etl`: all of it, shown under that context;
** *spread by usage*: shared out like overhead, in proportion to each group's usage-based costs that month;
** *shared*: assigned to no one, shown as `<shared>`;
* optionally *covers up to*: for a month-to-date bill, where its amounts stop. After that, the month is estimated.

All other lines appear under the cost `other`.

Saving a bill for a month that already has one replaces it. A line you leave empty is estimated from the pricing rules.

==== How a bill is shared

Each month is taken on its own (UTC). A topic's part of a line is its usage in the month divided by the month's usage, up to where the bill stops. For a date range that covers only part of a month, that is the usage in the range: 10 to 20 September gets the usage of those days divided by September's usage, times September's line. A range across months adds up each month's part of its own bill.

So costs follow how much was really used, not the number of days. Nothing needs to be recalculated when a bill is added or corrected: the costs are computed when you look at them. (Context rules are different: a change there needs a reprocess.)

If Kafka Cost Control has data for only part of a month, the month's bill is shared over what it has. A bill line for usage it hasn't measured at all goes to `other` as `<shared>`.

==== How costs are computed

Every place that shows a cost - Cost Overview, Explore, the unassigned topics and service accounts - reads the same view, `costs`, in the OLAP database. It gives each stored hour:

* in a month with a bill: the bill line's share by usage, as above; a metric that isn't one of the bill's lines costs nothing there, since everything else the provider billed is in the bill's other lines;
* otherwise - no bill, after a month-to-date bill stops, or a line the bill leaves empty - the pricing rule's cost for that hour, marked as estimated;
* and the bill's other lines and unmeasured lines, under `other`.

The view is rebuilt when the rules or bills change (at the latest after 10 seconds), so it always reflects them.

The OLAP export (`/olap/export`) reads the view too: each row has `cost`, `rate_cost` (the pricing rule's), `estimated` and `shared`, and the bill's other lines are rows under `other`.

The `aggregated` and `aggregated-table-friendly` topics carry usage only: their `cost` field stays in the schema but is empty. A consumer that wants costs reads them from Kafka Cost Control (the export or the GraphQL API). Versions before 0.11 filled it with the pricing rule's cost when the hour was processed, which a later bill or price change never updated.

==== Applying a bill's rates to the pricing rules

A list price rarely matches what the provider charges for what Kafka Cost Control measures: free allowances (Confluent's first partitions), storage billed per replica, metrics that count a little low. A bill already holds the real rates. On the *Bills* page, a bill's *Apply the bill's rates to the pricing rules* button shows, for each of its usage lines, the billed amount, the usage measured in the hours the bill covers, and the rate - amount divided by usage - as a price in the rule's unit, next to the current price.

Saving the ticked lines sets the pricing rules to these rates, by default as a *new price from the day the bill ends* (the 1st of the next month for a whole month): the hours after the bill are estimated at what the provider really charged, and earlier hours without a bill keep their prices. *Correct the current prices* applies them every day the current prices do instead. Hours a bill covers keep their billed cost either way. A rule keeps its unit and multiplier; its base cost is set to 0, since the rate alone reproduces the bill. A line without measured usage has no rate.

The rates are also available as a query: `billRates(month: "2026-10") { metricName amount usage costFactor }`, where `costFactor` is the cost per unit of the metric's raw value (per byte, per partition-hour).

==== Storage

Bills are kept in a compacted Kafka topic (`cc.topics.bills`, default `bills`), keyed by month. Kafka Cost Control creates it when it is missing; where its user may not create topics, create it beforehand with `cleanup.policy=compact` and one partition. Until the topic can be read, bills are unavailable and the rest of the application works as before.

===== Using GraphQL
[source,graphql]
----
mutation saveBill {
  saveBill(request: {month: "2026-09", networkWrite: 0.27, networkRead: 0.22, storage: 8.12, partitions: 22.50,
      otherLines: [{description: "Support", amount: 50, allocation: USAGE},
                   {description: "Connect", amount: 12, allocation: CONTEXT, context: [{key: "application", value: "etl"}]}]}) {
    month
    updatedBy
  }
}

query costs {
  billedCosts(request: {from: "2026-09-10T00:00:00Z", to: "2026-10-10T00:00:00Z", contextKeysToGroupBy: ["tenant"]}) {
    metrics { metric shares { name price estimatedPrice } }
    months { month billed billedUntil }
  }
}
----

`billedCosts` returns cents. `estimatedPrice` is the part of `price` that comes from the pricing rules.
